World shield#

All limits are per chunk and per second, and thread-safe for Folia. When TPS falls below world-shield.low-tps-threshold, every limit is multiplied by low-tps-multiplier.

Check Default Action
redstone 900 updates/chunk/s Freeze redstone in the chunk for 10 s (action: BREAK also breaks the trigger)
piston 120 moves/chunk/s Freeze pistons for 10 s
hopper 800 item moves/chunk/s Freeze hoppers for 5 s
explosion 40 primes/chunk/s Cancel extra explosions
entity-cap items 600, xp 200, vehicles 48, armor stands 48, falling blocks 120, projectiles 160, TNT 80, mobs 120, hanging 64 Cancel new spawns
chunk-ban container / player inventory 2 MB, item 400 KB, lectern book 120 KB (uncompressed sizes, checked on every insertion and book edit) Refuse the insertion
chunk-gen 120 new chunks/s server-wide while TPS is low Pause elytra firework boosts for 15 s

/og lag lists the heaviest loaded chunks to help you find the source.
