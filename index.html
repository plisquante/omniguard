Five layers, one plugin

Everything that can take a server down, covered.

Most servers run three or four separate plugins for this. OmniGuard replaces them with one engine that shares its knowledge: a flood seen by the network layer tightens the login guard, a punished player shows up in the ban-evasion map, and every decision lands in one alert stream.

Packet firewall

Checks run on the network thread, so malicious packets are dropped before the server does any work on them.

  • Book exploits, NaN and out-of-world movement
  • Click, slot and creative item exploits
  • Crash commands and selector bombs
  • Byte and frame floods cut before decoding

Real dupe detection

The Item Provenance Ledger gives every valuable item an invisible identity. Two copies of one identity means a dupe, whatever exploit made it.

  • Chain of custody for every item
  • Verified on the right thread (Folia-safe)
  • Gain monitor for diamonds and netherite
  • Patches for known dupe vectors

Lag-machine shield

Per-chunk limits for the contraptions that kill TPS. They tighten by themselves when the server struggles.

  • Redstone, piston and hopper floods
  • Entity caps (items, minecarts, frames…)
  • Explosion and chunk-generation floods
  • Chunk-ban and book-ban protection

Connection shield

Bot floods are absorbed and real players keep playing. Attack mode switches on and off by itself.

  • Connection and login floods
  • One-time reconnect check for unknown IPs
  • Ban-evasion links and rejoin cycling
  • One-command lockdown

Server hardening

The attacks that do not come through packets: a backdoored plugin quietly giving someone OP, an exploit kit reading your plugin list, a forgotten RCON password.

  • Untrusted operators de-opped within seconds
  • Plugin list and server version hidden from players
  • /og doctor audits your configuration
  • Illegal items detected (enchant 32767, 127-stacks)

Questions

Frequently asked.

Does OmniGuard replace my anticheat?

No. OmniGuard protects the server: crashes, dupes, lag, floods and takeovers. Run it next to your anticheat. Anticheats can even report their detections into OmniGuard's alerts and Discord through the API.

Ten people play from my house. Will they be kicked?

No. IPs that real players joined from are trusted: they get five times the limits and are never blocked. A whole IP is only blocked for an unmistakable flood. The test suite includes exactly this scenario.

What does it need?

Java 17+, a Paper, Purpur, Spigot or Folia server from 1.17.1 to 26.x, and the free PacketEvents plugin.

Can I try it without risk?

Yes. Turn on monitor-mode and OmniGuard detects and alerts on everything without blocking anything. Run /og doctor for a configuration check-up.

I am behind BungeeCord or Velocity.

It is detected automatically. Per-IP connection limits step aside, because all traffic comes from the proxy. Everything else keeps working with the forwarded player IPs.
